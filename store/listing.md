Play three beloved board games in one beautiful app, in English or አማርኛ.

ገበጣ — the relay-sowing seed game with families of four.
ዳማ — Ethiopian draughts: straight-line moves, flying kings, swipe to move. When a piece is crowned, your own hero emoji wears the crown!
ዱምና — dominoes with Draw and Block rules, to 50 or 100.

• Play the computer (easy, medium, hard) with hints — works offline
• Two players on one phone
• Quick online match or private room with a code, rating and leaderboards
• Relaxing Ethiopian-style music (masenqo & krar mood) — toggle any time
• Daily streaks, achievements and match history
• Pick a hero avatar: Lion of Judah, Queen of Sheba and more
